Week 4 - App Lab and Play Lab


Another enjoyable week this was! I found Play Lab and App Lab to be very easy to use, and I'm happy with the silly things I made with them. The first one is based off of a Hearts of Iron 4 mod called Equestria at war, which although My Little Pony themed is quite enjoyable; the humor in the quiz is probably not understandable to anyone unfamiliar with the mod and HoI4 in general. The second is it's own thing mostly, only the character's name is that of one of my stuffed animals.
