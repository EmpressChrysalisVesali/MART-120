Week 5 - Alice


Ah, this one. Perhaps this was the most difficult of all the weeks, as although I could understand how Alice worked, I found the process of working with it to be very tedious and draining and was also suffering from an inability to think of what to make. The code itself was kind of weird to work with and it did a lot of things I was not expecting it to do (such as the wall physics), which created a lot of hangups. In the end, I think I made an okay game!
