Week 6 - HTML (First Week)


I quite like HTML, I find it easy to use and satisfying. The first and third pages are self-explainatory really, the second one with the story perhaps seems random. That's because it's actually a section of a roleplay multiplayer game that I played with my friends over a year ago. I had it typed up and ready to use for when the time was right within the game, but sadly that time never arrived, so I never got to use this particular mini story. The idea was that the player who recieved this story would have to react to it in some way, and the scenario would continue on from there and either lead to them discovering that I was planning to invade a specific nation, giving them a chance to prevent me from doing so, or they would fail to discover this and simply come away confused until I commenced my invasion.
